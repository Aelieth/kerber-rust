# B1 — lib/krb5, lib/crypto, lib/gssapi, clients (the client side)

The client library (`lib/krb5/krb`, `ccache`, `keytab`, `os`), crypto
(`lib/crypto`), GSS and SPNEGO (`lib/gssapi`), the AP-REQ acceptor, the PAC
verifier, PKINIT's CMS parsing, and the client tools (`kinit`, `klist`,
`kvno`, `kdestroy`, `kswitch`, `kpasswd`). The FAST reply and expired-password
rows (`fast.c`, `get_in_tkt.c`, `gic_pwd.c`, `prompter.c`) have live cells on
the MIT 1.22.2 KDC behind `scripts/lib/kdc-rewrite-proxy.py`, with MIT
`kinit -T` and Rust `krb5-kinit --fast` side by side
(`scripts/mit-fast-kdc-gate.sh`).
