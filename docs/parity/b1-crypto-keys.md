# B1 — the keys and keyed checksums lib/crypto makes (des3-cbc-sha1)

The keys `krb5_c_make_random_key` draws and `krb5_c_random_to_key` shapes, and the keyed checksum `krb5int_dk_checksum` computes. des3-cbc-sha1 is where they differ from the other enctypes: its 24-octet key is 21 random octets spread over three DES keys with odd parity on every octet, and its key derivation (DK) runs the 3DES block cipher, not AES. AES, rc4-hmac and Camellia take their random octets as the key. The cipher state between messages is in [b1-crypto-state.md](b1-crypto-state.md), the rest of the crypto in [b1-client.md](b1-client.md).
