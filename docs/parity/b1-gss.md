# B1 — lib/gssapi (the GSS-API krb5 mechanism and SPNEGO)

The GSS-API rows of B1: the acceptor's context and token checks, wrap,
unwrap and IOV, the sequence-number window, SPNEGO's negotiation,
`gss_display_status` and the credential store. They moved here byte for byte
from [b1-client.md](b1-client.md), which keeps the rest of the client library;
the client tools' rows are in [b1-tools.md](b1-tools.md).
