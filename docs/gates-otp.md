The row of the OTP gate, which never stood in [gates.md](gates.md) (its 60 KiB is full). It runs MIT `kinit -T`
and the Rust `kinit` (copied into the container as `kinit`, so its messages carry MIT's name) cell for cell against
MIT's KDC and its `otp` module.
